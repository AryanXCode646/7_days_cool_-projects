DAY 05

AirDraw Pro — Touchless Presentation Whiteboard

A professional touchless whiteboard tool that uses hand gestures to draw in mid-air, automatically beautifies hand-drawn shapes, and exports high-res presentation diagrams.

✍️ Live In-Browser Whiteboard (Draw rough circles or boxes to auto-snap!)

Everyday Solutions & Key Features

✨ Geometric Shape Auto-Beautifier

Press A to automatically snap rough hand-drawn circles, rectangles, and straight lines into crisp vector shapes.

📋 Whiteboard & Darkboard Canvas Modes

Press W for a high-contrast presentation whiteboard or B for darkboard mode suitable for streaming and lectures.

↩️ Multi-Level Undo & Redo

Full stroke history support allowing you to undo (Z) or redo (Y) mistakes during live presentations.

Gesture & Keyboard Commands

Key / GestureAction
☝️ Index FingerDraw on canvas in mid-air
✌️ Peace (2 Fingers)Selection / Hover mode (move pointer without drawing)
✊ FistEraser brush
AToggle Shape Auto-Beautifier (snaps rough shapes to geometric forms)
W / BToggle Presentation Whiteboard / Darkboard
Z / YUndo / Redo last drawn stroke
+ / -Increase / Decrease brush diameter
SExport high-resolution PNG snapshot to disk
