DAY 04

Ergonomics, Blink Rate & Privacy Avatar

A smart desk-health monitor that detects poor sitting posture, tracks eye blink fatigue (20-20-20 rule), and offers a particle-based Privacy Avatar for anonymous video calls.

Everyday Health Solutions

🪑 Real-Time Slouching & Head Tilt Detector

Uses computer vision facial landmarks to calculate head tilt angle and distance from the screen, providing instant color-coded posture feedback.

👁️ Blink Rate & 20-20-20 Eye Fatigue Monitor

Monitors Eye Aspect Ratio (EAR) to count blinks per minute and notifies you when it's time to rest your eyes.

🎭 Privacy Avatar Anonymizer Mode

Press A to transform your raw webcam feed into a stylized particle avatar, preserving your privacy during meetings or recordings.

Keyboard Controls

KeyAction
AToggle Privacy Avatar Anonymizer mode (replaces webcam with particle avatar)
SSave snapshot to disk
ESCExit application

python Day_4/main.py
