DAY 07

Digital Soul — Multimodal AI Desk Assistant

An intelligent desk companion combining vision object recognition, voice task dictation (auto-appends to daily_notes.txt), Pomodoro study timers, and offline/Gemini AI fallback.

Everyday Solutions & Key Features

📝 Spoken Daily Notes & Task Dictation

Say "take note [your text]" and the assistant automatically timestamps and saves your thought into daily_notes.txt.

⏱️ Pomodoro Study Timer

Say "start pomodoro" or "focus timer" to start a 25-minute deep focus study session.

🧠 Offline + Gemini Multimodal Intelligence

Powered by Google Gemini 1.5 Flash, OpenAI API, and a built-in offline smart assistant for time, date, and study motivation without any API keys.

Voice Commands Cheat-Sheet

Voice Command (English / Hindi)Assistant Action
"take note [text]" / "likh lo [text]"Saves and timestamps note to daily_notes.txt
"read notes" / "what are my tasks"Reads out your recent saved notes
"start pomodoro" / "focus timer"Initiates 25-minute study block
"what is the time" / "samay kya hai"Announces current exact time
"what do you see" / "kya dikh raha hai"Multimodal webcam object identification

Running Locally

python Day_7/main.py
