DAY 07_2

Virtual Piano & Music Learning Studio

A full 12-tone chromatic software synthesizer with real-time chord detection HUD, practice metronome with adjustable BPM, and multi-track WAV audio export.

🎹 Live Web Audio Piano (Press A, S, D, F, G, H, J, K on your keyboard!)

Everyday Solutions & Key Features

🎼 Real-Time Chord Detection

Press C to toggle the chord analyzer that recognizes Major, Minor, and Seventh chords when played together.

⏱️ Practice Metronome

Press M to toggle a metronome and adjust tempo with [ and ] from 40 to 240 BPM.

🎙️ Multi-Track WAV Audio Exporter

Record your performances with R and export them into high-quality WAV audio files with S.
