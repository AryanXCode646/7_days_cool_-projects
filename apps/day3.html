DAY 03

Swarm Ecosystem & Genetic Problem Solver

Interactive biological ecosystem simulator with genetic adaptation, obstacle painting, food spawning, disease contagion modeling, and telemetry data export.

🐜 Live Swarm Simulation (Click canvas to spawn food!)

Everyday Solutions & Features

🧱 Interactive Obstacle Maze Painting

Right-click and drag anywhere on the canvas to paint walls, testing how autonomous agents solve pathfinding dilemmas.

🧬 Genetic Evolution & Lineage HUD

Agents pass down traits (speed, sensory radius, metabolic efficiency, immunity) across generations in real time.

🦠 Contagion & Herd Immunity

Press E to introduce an epidemiological contagion and watch biological resistance develop through natural selection.

📊 CSV Telemetry Exporter

Press X to export agent population size, mean speed, average age, and mutation rate to swarm_telemetry.csv.

Controls & Mouse Commands

InputAction
Left ClickPlace fresh food caches for the swarm
Right Click + DragDraw obstacle / maze walls to test agent navigation
Middle ClickSpawn new agents with randomized genomes
EToggle Epidemic Contagion & Antibody Simulation
CClear all drawn obstacle walls
XExport population metrics to CSV file

Running Locally

python Day_3/Artificial_Life_Simulation.py
